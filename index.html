About

Medical imaging researcher & builder.

Tanzilal Mustaqim is a researcher in Computer Vision and Deep Learning focused on medical image analysis. His master's thesis at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) developed detection of Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia subtypes on microscopic blood images, and he now continues his research at Telkom University on diabetic retinopathy and liver disease classification.

With a strong foundation in sentiment analysis, text mining, and predictive machine learning, he bridges research and product. As a Full-Stack Developer and PHP Developer, he builds real-world applications — clinic systems, attendance, logistics, and AI services powered by RAG — using a modern stack including Next.js, NestJS, and FastAPI.
